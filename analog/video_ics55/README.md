single-cell layout closes LVS and the partial DRC.**  The three recorded

| blocker | state |

## Cell study (device level, no parasitics)

`analog/video/pdk_cell.py ics55` now runs the same P5 method as gf180/IHP:
255 unit cells + diode-connected replica, 37.5 ohm and 2 pF load, 20 ps edge,
25 ps bit skew, 40 ns period.  The ics55 entry uses the 1.2 V core devices
(`pm1p2_svt_lp` / `nm1p2_svt_lp`) with mirror 8/1, cascode 8/0.5, pair 4/0.28:

```
ics55: vdd 1.2 V, mirror (8.0, 1.0), cascode (8.0, 0.5), pair (4.0, 0.28)
  127 -> 128:   127.953 LSB (err   -0.047), settle  2.03 ns, dvbp  -0.00 mV, dvcp  -0.00 mV
    0 -> 255:   254.561 LSB (err   -0.439), settle  2.41 ns, dvbp  +0.12 mV, dvcp  +0.30 mV
  dc: vbp 0.6134 V, vcp 0.0196 V, mirror drain 0.6146 V, tail 0.0836 V
ics55: PASS  (worst 0.439 LSB, bias 0.30 mV)
```

That is the first ics55 DAC result: carry inside 0.05 LSB, full scale inside
0.44 LSB, bias excursion 0.3 mV, settling 2.4 ns at 25 MS/s.

Bias point (`vcp`): at 1.2 V the series replica forces
`vcp = Vdd - Vsg(mirror) - Vsg(cascode) = 19.6 mV`.  That is an *internal*
node generated by the on-chip replica from the 16 uA `Iref`; no external
20 mV reference is needed.  It is, however, the supply-margin limit.  A
supply sweep at tt (`pdk_cell.py ics55 --vdd ... --corner tt`) gives:

| Vdd | vbp | vcp | carry | full scale | settle | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1.14 V | 0.553 | **-0.040** | -0.069 | -0.489 | inf | **FAIL** |
| 1.20 V | 0.613 | 0.020 | -0.047 | -0.439 | 2.03 / 2.41 ns | PASS |
| 1.26 V | 0.673 | 0.080 | -0.027 | -0.391 | 2.07 / 2.52 ns | PASS |

At 1.14 V the replica wants a negative cascode gate, which the current sink
cannot produce; the full-scale step never enters the 0.5 LSB window.  The
gate now fails on a negative `vcp` or a missing settle time, so this cannot
slip through as a PASS.  At 1.2 V the full-scale margin to the 0.5 LSB limit
is only 0.061 LSB, so the bias generator (not the mirror sizing) is the
remaining ics55 design risk.

Widening the cell to buy headroom was measured and rejected:

| mirror | cascode | vcp | carry | full scale | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 8/1 | 8/0.5 | 0.020 | +0.047 | **-0.439** | PASS |
| 32/1 | 16/0.5 | 0.191 | +0.008 | -0.566 | FAIL (FS, settle inf) |
| 64/1 | 32/0.5 | 0.274 | +0.050 | -0.849 | FAIL (FS, settle inf, bias 1.0 mV) |

The wider devices load `vbp`/`vcp` with 4-8x the gate capacitance and the
full-scale step no longer settles inside 40 ns, so the row keeps the 8/1
sizing and the replica is the bias generator.

## What the PCell gives now

`libs.tech/klayout/pcells/ics55_mos_analog.py` (PDK commit `b756fc8`) draws,
for `fingers=m`:

* one shared active strip, `m+1` contacts on a 0.26 um pitch
  (`CT_S_4` 0.055 + CT 0.09 + 0.055), 0.03 active enclosure;
* a poly bus tying every finger, one gate contact on a 0.17 um pad;
* one M1 island per contact with interdigitated straps (even to a bus below,
  odd to a bus above) and a separate gate island (0.042 um^2).

For `fingers=1` the cell is 0.41 um wide (`L + 2*0.205`) and `W` tall, with
two S/D islands at x = +/-0.13 um and the gate island above the active.  The
array plan is the same as gf180/sky130: one row of 255 cells on a 7.5 um
pitch, `d_`/`db_` pads on a 3.75 um grid, which is what the fixed cell has to
be placed on.

## Single-cell layout

`gen_cell.py` places the four devices with the PDK PCell, wires them on M1/M2
(`cd` needs one M2 crossing with 0.09 V1 / 0.17 M1 pad / 0.13 M2), labels the
nine ports on M1, and writes `build/cell.gds`.  `cell.cdl` is the matching
schematic (the PMOS bulk is written as the same `vdd` net as its source; the
deck ties the n-well to the power alias).

The ics55 LVS deck returns:

```
.SUBCKT CELL cd d db idump iout md vbp vcp vdd
M$1 iout  d  cd  VSS NM1P2_SVT_LP L=0.28U W=4U
M$2 idump db cd  VSS NM1P2_SVT_LP L=0.28U W=4U
M$3 md    vbp vdd vdd PM1P2_SVT_LP L=1U   W=8U
M$4 cd    vcp md  vdd PM1P2_SVT_LP L=0.5U W=8U
```

`ICS55_LVS_COMPARE=PASS` with `-rd strict_topology=true` (the default
comparer trips over the well-tied bulk net; the strict path resolves it by
pin name).  The partial KLayout DRC reports 0 items with `no_density=true`
(the density windows are 100 um squares, meaningless for a 5 um cell).

## Row: 255 cells + replica + 510 pads

`gen_gds.py` builds the row on the same plan as the other leaves: 255 cells
on a 7.5 um pitch, `d_`/`db_` pads on a 3.75 um grid (pad j at 2.25 + j*3.75),
510 M2 pads, 1912.5 um wide.  The cell is the one above; the replica sits
right of the row with its mirror/cascode diode-connected and an `iref` pad on
`vcp` (the bias is generated on-chip from the 16 uA reference).

Routing: `vdd`/`vbp`/`vcp` on M2 above the row, `iout`/`idump` on M2 below,
M1 stubs and V1 onto each bus, the pair gates fanned out on M1 to the pads
(V1 at the pad).  The per-cell `cd` route is M2 and stays clear of the gate
fanout.

Results on `build/video_ics55.gds`:

| check | result |
|---|---|
| partial DRC (`ics55_drc.drc`, `no_density=true`) | **0 items** |
| hand rules (`check_rules.py`: CT size/space, CT-gate, AA/poly/M1 enclosure, AA/PO width) | **PASS** |
| LVS extraction | 1022 devices, 515 ports |
| LVS compare (`strict_topology=true`) | **ICS55_LVS_COMPARE=PASS** |
| netlist structure (`check_netlist.py`) | **PASS** (chains, drains, 510 distinct gate pads, replica diode) |

`gen_cdl.py` emits the matching schematic (`build/video_ics55.cdl`); the
`M` prefix matters - `X` devices are read as subcircuit calls and the compare
then sees no MOS at all.

## Next step

1. Carry on the extracted netlist: `extracted_carry.py` runs it on the
   KLayout LVS extraction (`row_ext.cir`, converted M->X for the subcircuit
   models):

   ```
   127 -> 128:   127.951 LSB (err   -0.049)
     0 -> 255:   254.561 LSB (err   -0.439)
   ```

   i.e. the layout's devices reproduce the schematic study.  The Magic PEX
   of the full row does not finish in a practical time (flat: >60 min;
   hierarchical with `drc off`: >40 min), so the *parasitic* carry is still
   open; `pex_carry.py` is the runner for it once the extraction is solved.
2. PDK-side: the DRC deck still needs the FEOL/BEOL width/space families
   (see `docs/ics55_pdk_repair.md`); the hand checks cover the device rules
   in the meantime.
3. Wrapper: the 510 pads, `iref`, `vdd`/`vbp`/`vcp`/`iout`/`idump` and the
   bias decoupling are the class-C work left.

The pre-fix record (M1 over the whole active, "no layout can pass LVS") is in
the git history of this file; that geometry no longer exists in the PDK.
