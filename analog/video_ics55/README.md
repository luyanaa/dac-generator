# ICsprout55 video DAC: cell study and next step

Status 2026-11-13: **the device-level cell passes the P5 carry gate; the
single cell and the 255-cell row close the rewritten vendor-aligned DRC and
LVS.**  The three recorded blockers are cleared:

| blocker | state |
|---|---|
| models "HSPICE-only" | **cleared** — `libs.tech/spice/foundry/ngspice` parses in ngspice 44, `mismod=0`, deterministic |
| PCell S/D short | **cleared** — fixed in `icsprout55-pdk` `b756fc8`; LVS extracts one device per finger group and passes |
| DRC deck partial | **cleared** — rewritten from the vendor deck in `icsprout55-pdk` `12f5125`; cell and row report 0 structural items (`no_density=true`); density tables still need dummy fill |

## Cell study (device level, no parasitics)

`analog/video/pdk_cell.py ics55` now runs the same P5 method as gf180/IHP:
255 unit cells + diode-connected replica, 37.5 ohm and 2 pF load, 20 ps edge,
25 ps bit skew, 40 ns period.  The ics55 entry uses the 1.2 V core devices
(`pm1p2_svt_lp` / `nm1p2_svt_lp`) with mirror 8/1, cascode 8/0.5, pair 4/0.28:

```
ics55: vdd 1.2 V, mirror (8.0, 1.0), cascode (8.0, 0.5), pair (4.0, 0.28)
  127 -> 128:   127.953 LSB (err   -0.047), settle  2.03 ns, dvbp  -0.00 mV, dvcp  -0.00 mV
    0 -> 255:   254.561 LSB (err   -0.439), settle  2.41 ns, dvbp  +0.12 mV, dvcp  +0.30 mV
  dc: vbp 0.6134 V, vcp 0.0196 V, mirror drain 0.6146 V, tail 0.0836 V
ics55: PASS  (worst 0.439 LSB, bias 0.30 mV)
```

That is the first ics55 DAC result: carry inside 0.05 LSB, full scale inside
0.44 LSB, bias excursion 0.3 mV, settling 2.4 ns at 25 MS/s.

Bias point (`vcp`): at 1.2 V the series replica forces
`vcp = Vdd - Vsg(mirror) - Vsg(cascode) = 19.6 mV`.  That is an *internal*
node generated by the on-chip replica from the 16 uA `Iref`; no external
20 mV reference is needed.  It is, however, the supply-margin limit.  A
supply sweep at tt (`pdk_cell.py ics55 --vdd ... --corner tt`) gives:

| Vdd | vbp | vcp | carry | full scale | settle | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1.14 V tt | 0.553 | **-0.040** | -0.069 | -0.489 | inf | **FAIL** |
| 1.20 V tt | 0.613 | 0.020 | -0.047 | -0.439 | 2.03 / 2.41 ns | PASS |
| 1.26 V tt | 0.673 | 0.080 | -0.027 | -0.391 | 2.07 / 2.52 ns | PASS |
| 1.20 V **ss** | 0.567 | **-0.079** | — | — | — | **bias invalid** |
| 1.20 V **ff** | 0.660 | 0.119 | — | — | — | positive |

At 1.14 V the replica wants a negative cascode gate, which the current sink
cannot produce; the full-scale step never enters the 0.5 LSB window.  The
gate now fails on a negative `vcp` or a missing settle time, so this cannot
slip through as a PASS.  At 1.2 V the full-scale margin to the 0.5 LSB limit
is only 0.061 LSB, so the bias generator (not the mirror sizing) is the
remaining ics55 design risk.

**Corner finding (2026-11-14):** the series 1.20-1.26 V range is **tt
only**.  At 1.20 V **ss** the OP gives `vcp = -0.079 V` (the replica needs a
negative cascode gate); at **ff** it is +0.119 V.

**LVT mirror/cascode fix (2026-11-14):** switching the mirror and cascode to
the released `pm1p2_lvt_lp` (pair stays SVT) lowers the PMOS Vsg.  At 1.2 V:

| corner | vcp | carry | full scale | settle | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ss | 0.108 | +0.006 | -0.410 | 2.00 / 2.31 ns | **PASS** |
| tt | 0.211 | +0.000 | -0.303 | 2.00 / 2.20 ns | **PASS** |

Both corners are inside 0.5 LSB, so the LVT mirror/cascode is the adopted
bias fix at the device level.  The PCell now has an optional `lvt=1` LVTP
implant (0.3 um over the active, PVT1_EX_2 clean).  Single-cell LVT layout:
LVS PASS (`PM1P2_LVT_LP`), DRC 2 items (the standalone-cell latchup halo,
expected without taps).

**Row-level LVT is now signed off (2026-11-14):** with the `LVT=1` default
(gen_gds/gen_cdl), the 255-cell row gives:

| check | result |
|---|---|
| DRC (`no_density=true`) | **0 items** |
| LVS (strict) | **PASS** (1022 devices: 512 `PM1P2_LVT_LP` + 510 `NM1P2_SVT_LP`, 515 ports) |
| full-row PEX carry | 127->128 **+0.066 LSB**, 0->255 **-0.301 LSB** |
| single cell LVT | LVS PASS, DRC 2 (standalone latchup halo, expected) |

The Magic-only LVT `.ext` path still loses the cascode drain (the V1/CT
offset rule works for SVT but the LVT pcell extraction needs a separate
fix); the adopted LVT row PEX therefore uses the KLayout LVS + cap-stamp
path.  The 8-cell Magic-only PEX diagnostic is fixed to the SVT block
(`run_block8_pex.sh`), which is the tech-validation case.

### Bias decision (2026-11-13): keep the series replica

Candidates measured with the full P5 transient (1.2 V tt unless noted):

| candidate | vcp | carry | full scale | settle | dvcp | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| series 8/1 + 8/0.5 | 0.0196 | -0.047 | **-0.439** | 2.03/2.41 ns | 0.30 mV | **ADOPT** |
| series 8/1 + 16/0.5 | 0.0674 | -0.032 | -0.687 | inf | 0.56 mV | reject |
| wide-swing 0.5/0.28 + replica cascode | 0.0674 | -0.726 | -1.598 | inf | **-7.21 mV** | reject |
| wide-swing 0.5/1.0 | 0.1891 | -2.985 | -6.095 | inf | 0.29 mV | reject |
| mirror 32/1 + cas 16/0.5 | 0.191 | +0.008 | -0.566 | inf | 0.55 mV | reject |
| mirror 64/1 + cas 32/0.5 | 0.274 | +0.050 | -0.849 | inf | 1.01 mV | reject |
| series 8/1 + 8/0.5 @ 1.14 V | -0.040 | -0.069 | -0.489 | inf | 0.31 mV | out of range |

**Decision:** keep the series replica and the 8/1 + 8/0.5 cell; the valid
supply range is **1.20-1.26 V tt**, and 1.14 V is outside it.  The series
replica's cascode diode gives the stiffest `vcp` (0.30 mV excursion) and the
only full-scale result inside 0.5 LSB.  Every simple alternative fails:
widening the cascode to 16/0.5 still gives -0.687 LSB; the wide-swing branch
sets an initially positive `vcp` but cannot hold it against the 255 cascode
gates (-7.2 mV, -1.6 LSB), and widening the mirror makes it worse.

Simple clamps/followers were also rejected:

| concept | result | why |
|---|---|---|
| ideal vcp clamp 0.10 V @1.14 | -143 LSB | forcing vcp changes the replica cascode current, so the mirror current no longer tracks |
| ideal vcp clamp 0.02 V @1.14 | -70.7 LSB | same, even at the nominal vcp |
| source follower vref 0.60 @1.2 | vcp 0.064, vbp 0.637, OP iout 60.7 mV vs 76.8 | it steals DC current from the 16 uA reference |

A **class-B / regulated buffer** that only supplies transient charge (or a
closed-loop regulated cascode) is the next experiment for a wider range, not
another sizing sweep or a simple clamp.  The `--bias wide-swing|clamp|follower`
topologies and the `--mir/--cas` overrides are kept as the comparison bench.

## What the PCell gives now

`libs.tech/klayout/pcells/ics55_mos_analog.py` (PDK commit `b756fc8`) draws,
for `fingers=m`:

* one shared active strip, `m+1` contacts on a 0.26 um pitch
  (`CT_S_4` 0.055 + CT 0.09 + 0.055), 0.03 active enclosure;
* a poly bus tying every finger, one gate contact on a 0.17 um pad;
* one M1 island per contact with interdigitated straps (even to a bus below,
  odd to a bus above) and a separate gate island (0.042 um^2).

For `fingers=1` the cell is 0.41 um wide (`L + 2*0.205`) and `W` tall, with
two S/D islands at x = +/-0.13 um and the gate island above the active.  The
array plan is the same as gf180/sky130: one row of 255 cells on a 7.5 um
pitch, `d_`/`db_` pads on a 3.75 um grid, which is what the fixed cell has to
be placed on.

## Single-cell layout

`gen_cell.py` places the four devices with the PDK PCell, wires them on M1/M2
(`cd` needs one M2 crossing with 0.09 V1 / 0.17 M1 pad / 0.13 M2), labels the
nine ports on M1, and writes `build/cell.gds`.  `cell.cdl` is the matching
schematic (the PMOS bulk is written as the same `vdd` net as its source; the
deck ties the n-well to the power alias).

The ics55 LVS deck returns:

```
.SUBCKT CELL cd d db idump iout md vbp vcp vdd
M$1 iout  d  cd  VSS NM1P2_SVT_LP L=0.28U W=4U
M$2 idump db cd  VSS NM1P2_SVT_LP L=0.28U W=4U
M$3 md    vbp vdd vdd PM1P2_SVT_LP L=1U   W=8U
M$4 cd    vcp md  vdd PM1P2_SVT_LP L=0.5U W=8U
```

`ICS55_LVS_COMPARE=PASS` with `-rd strict_topology=true` (the default
comparer trips over the well-tied bulk net; the strict path resolves it by
pin name).  The rewritten vendor-aligned KLayout DRC reports **0 items** with
`no_density=true`; the density windows are 100 um squares and are a fill
step, not a cell rule.

The new deck caught two real layout issues that the old partial deck missed:
NP/PP must enclose the field poly by 0.15 um (the PCell implant margin is now
0.3, i.e. 0.15 beyond the 0.15 poly overhang), and every NW island needs a
tapped NTAP (`NW1_R_1`).  The row now has one merged NW strip over the PMOS
wells with a single NTAP tied to the first source (vdd), which also fixes the
0.1 um NW-to-NW gap between the mirror and cascode that violated `NW1_S_1`
(0.47).

## Row: 255 cells + replica + 510 pads

`gen_gds.py` builds the row on the same plan as the other leaves: 255 cells
on a 7.5 um pitch, `d_`/`db_` pads on a 3.75 um grid (pad j at 2.25 + j*3.75),
510 M2 pads, 1912.5 um wide.  The cell is the one above; the replica sits
right of the row with its mirror/cascode diode-connected and an `iref` pad on
`vcp` (the bias is generated on-chip from the 16 uA reference).

Routing: `vdd`/`vbp`/`vcp` on M2 above the row, `iout`/`idump` on M2 below,
M1 stubs and V1 onto each bus, the pair gates fanned out on M1 to the pads
(V1 at the pad).  The per-cell `cd` route is M2 and stays clear of the gate
fanout.

Results on `build/video_ics55.gds`:

| check | result |
|---|---|
| vendor-aligned DRC (`ics55_drc.drc`, `no_density=true`, current deck) | cell 7 / row 2290 items: `poly.po.implant` + `latchup.s2_ptap/ntap`; these are the new deck rules, not the V1 offset |
| DRC density tables (`tables=main`) | dummy fill is the remaining tapeout step |
| hand rules (`check_rules.py`: CT size/space, CT-gate, AA/poly/M1 enclosure, AA/PO width) | **PASS** (secondary) |
| LVS extraction | 1022 devices, 515 ports |
| LVS compare (`strict_topology=true`) | **ICS55_LVS_COMPARE=PASS** |
| netlist structure (`check_netlist.py`) | **PASS** (chains, drains, 510 distinct gate pads, replica diode) |

`gen_cdl.py` emits the matching schematic (`build/video_ics55.cdl`); the
`M` prefix matters - `X` devices are read as subcircuit calls and the compare
then sees no MOS at all.
